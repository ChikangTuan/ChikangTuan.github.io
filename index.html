个人简介AboutAbout

我是段继罡，首都师范大学数学科学学院博士研究生，导师为赵星教授。我的研究主要围绕计算机断层成像（CT）的数学方法与图像处理展开。

我关注如何结合成像物理、优化方法与深度学习，从含噪声、信息不完整或受伪影影响的测量中恢复可靠的 CT 图像。目前的研究涉及能谱与光子计数 CT、伪影校正，以及基于流匹配的图像重建。

我也参与 CT 系统搭建、实验数据采集与研究生讨论班，乐于将成像理论与实际问题相结合。

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Capital Normal University, supervised by Prof. Xing Zhao. My research focuses on mathematical methods for computed tomography (CT) and image processing.

I study how imaging physics, optimization, and deep learning can be combined to recover reliable CT images from noisy, incomplete, or artifact-corrupted measurements. My current interests include spectral and photon-counting CT, artifact correction, and flow matching for image reconstruction.

I also work on CT system development and experimental data acquisition, and help organize graduate seminars on CT imaging.

研究方向ResearchResearch Interests

  • 能谱与光子计数 CT：基物质分解、成像建模与定量重建。
  • CT 伪影校正：环状伪影与金属伪影的建模和校正，兼顾伪影去除与结构保真。
  • 生成式图像重建：面向低剂量、稀疏视角和有限角 CT 的流匹配方法。
  • Spectral and photon-counting CT: basis material decomposition, imaging models, and quantitative reconstruction.
  • CT artifact correction: modeling and correcting ring and metal artifacts while preserving structural detail.
  • Generative image reconstruction: flow matching for low-dose, sparse-view, and limited-angle CT.

讨论班与讲义Seminar & NotesSeminar & Lecture Notes

我参与组织研究生 CT 讨论班，并编写面向初学者的 CT 成像讲义，希望将成像理论、数值算法与实际应用联系起来，帮助新同学建立对 CT 成像的整体认识。

I help organize graduate seminars on CT imaging and write introductory lecture notes. The goal is to connect imaging theory, numerical methods, and practical applications, and to help new students build a coherent understanding of CT.
